# IDEA-004 — Ball-rain easter egg / author reveal

Status: open

## Product intent

Create a rare emotional easter egg that reveals an author block after an intentional repeated interaction.

## Implementation hypotheses

- A repeated-tap trigger followed by falling tennis balls.
- An author block revealed after the effect.
- Manual discovery or an optional controlled scroll; automatic scroll is not assumed.

Trigger, tap count, physics, duration, dismissal and scroll behavior are undecided.

## Why

A rare hidden moment can add personality without slowing normal match entry.

## Relevant when

- author/about surface;
- rare delight;
- optional effects infrastructure.

## Done when

The trigger is discoverable enough but rare; effect is interruptible, isolated, lazy-loaded and cleaned up; it respects reduced motion and does not steal scroll control.

## Evidence

Not implemented.
